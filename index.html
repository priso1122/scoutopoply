⚙️ GAME MASTER CONTROLS

📜 OFFICIAL SCOUTOPOLY RULES

OBJECTIVE: Build the strongest Scout Kingdom by acquiring bases, completing nighttime challenges, and demonstrating superior Scout Spirit and Honor.


STARTING SETUP: Each patrol starts with 500 Trail Points (TP).


HONOR SYSTEM:

  • +10 Honor: Helping another patrol voluntarily
  • +5 Honor: Exceptional teamwork
  • -20 Honor: Cheating or dangerous behavior
  • -10 Honor: Breaking a promise/trade

FINAL SCORE FORMULA:

Final Score = Trail Points + Owned Base Values + (Honor Score × 10)

🏕️ PATROL HOW-TO GUIDE

1. ROLL & MOVE: Patrol Leader rolls the dice and moves token around central board.

2. RESOLVE SPACE: Buy base, pay rent, draw card, or visit real-world station.

3. BUILD & TRADE: Upgrade bases or trade with other patrols.

4. SAFETY FIRST: No running in the dark without flashlights. Unsafe behavior = Reflection (Jail)!
