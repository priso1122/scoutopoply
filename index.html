❓ How to Play Scoutopoly

Scoutopoly is a multiplayer Scout-themed property board game[cite: 3] inspired by classic Monopoly gameplay[cite: 3].

  • 1. Roll Two Dice: On your turn, roll two 6-sided dice (total 2–12)[cite: 3]. Rolling doubles gives you an extra turn[cite: 3]! Rolling doubles 3 times sends you to Camp Penalty[cite: 3].
  • 2. Movement & Base Camp: Move your Scout token step by step around the 40 board spaces[cite: 3]. Passing or landing on 🏕️ Base Camp awards +200 Scout Coins[cite: 3].
  • 3. Properties & Rent: Buy unowned Scout locations when you land on them[cite: 3]. When other players land on your locations, they automatically pay you rent[cite: 3]!
  • 4. Upgrades: Own a full property color group to upgrade your sites from Empty Site up to ⛺ Tent, 🏕️ Camp, 🔥 Advanced Camp, 🏠 Scout Base, and ⭐ Grand Scout Base to increase rent[cite: 3].
  • 5. Special Spaces: Land on 🏅 Scout Badge or 🧭 Scout Challenge for random bonuses or penalties[cite: 3]. Transportation and Utility spaces scale in rent based on ownership count[cite: 3].
  • 6. Camp Penalty: Avoid ⛺ Camp Penalty! Pay a release fee, roll doubles, or use an escape card to get out[cite: 3].
  • 7. Victory: Manage your Scout Coins wisely, avoid bankruptcy, and be the last remaining player to win the Scoutopoly Championship[cite: 3]!
